I would personally choose shared autonomy for both a routine handover and mid-task changes. Shared autonomy allows relatively moderate control over the robot without it being fully problematic that requires your full attention when performing the task with some amount of confidence. Using manual control might gave the user the most freedom, but it also comes with difficulty of controlling the robot and might overlook some aspects or features. On the other hand, fully autonomy might be problematic with little to no human correction in case of a route traveling problem for example. My voice redesign recommended a safety warning or confirmation regarding the action of cancelling the current task that might resulted in a hazardous action.
