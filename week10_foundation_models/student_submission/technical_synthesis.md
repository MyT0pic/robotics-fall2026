Turning a model's response into useful robot behavior is quite complicated. The prompt may need to have some sort of background information of everything necessary in the involved task and the prompt instruction needs to balance between clearness and relatively detailed without overcomplication for the LLM. Take mission 1, for example in regard to navigation, a clear request needs a revision of an additional background information of the starting position was sufficient enough to carry out the request without making potentially incorrect assumption of the task. In the case of mission 2, for deciding a tool based on a image, although the revised prompt included the purpose of the tool to a problem along with a clear tool identification question, the response yielded an incorrect tool; most likely due to the prompt unable to clearly identify the compass within the provided image. Navigating inside a maze might be too complicated for the LLM to resolve, as it almost for certain get stuck somewhere despite a clear linear directional instruction toward the goal is provided.
