Trying to incorporate LLM into the robot's behavior decision is a very complicated and potentially very infuriating process. Occasionally, it's very difficult to pinpoint or narrow down the possible problem when trying to resolve them when it's separated distinctly between the human prompt structure category and the LLM decision and thought process category. I would say the most challenging aspect is to balance the human and LLM distinct challenges. Humans are often lazy and speak in few and vague words that we would generally understand based on the context by our five senses, but LLM have difficulty generally understanding the vagueness of these prompts without additional observation contexts which might led to prolong clarifications and repetitiveness which might annoy the human-end experience due to lack of tolerance for patient. In the future, it's very unlikely that I would incorporate LLM based decision-making as a fundamental feature in robotics or computing related product, but perhaps useful for the development side for catching error or concept related learning.
